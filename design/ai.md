# Azizbek — tanlov dizaynlarini yangi AI bilan ishlatish

2026-10-15. Avval tanlangan dizaynning sahifasi yoki ai.md faylini o‘qi; barcha 11 manbani birga kontekstga yuklama. Unda tanlov konteksti, visual contract, run, haqiqiy source, oldingi tuzatishlar va xatoni tekshirish/tuzatish tartibi bor.

- [KasbLab](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/kasblab/) — /design/kasblab/; Oq fon, katta sarlavha, ko‘k aksent va kurs kartalari. Toza, ishonchli mahsulot dizayni.
- [Samar](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/samar/) — /design/samar/; Issiq krem fon, serif sarlavhalar, yashil aksent va Samarqand me’moriy illyustratsiyasi.
- [Orbit](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/orbit/) — /design/orbit/; Qora fon, yirik tipografika, yashil aksent va geometrik kompozitsiya. Kuchli vizual taassurot.
- [Forma](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/forma/) — /design/forma/; Sokin ranglar, mahsulot illyustratsiyalari va tartibli katalog. Premium do‘kon ko‘rinishi.
- [Pulseboard](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/pulse/) — /design/pulse/; Yon menyu, ko‘rsatkichlar, grafik va buyurtmalar jadvali. Ko‘p ma’lumot uchun qulay interfeys.
- [Qadam · Material](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/qadam/) — /design/qadam/; Google Sans Flex, tonal ranglar, yumaloq kartalar va katta pill tugmalar. Hozirgi Google dizayn yo‘nalishidan ilhomlangan.
- [Lola](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/lola/) — /design/lola/; Fraunces serif va DM Sans juftligi. Tiniq terrakota, krem va och yashil; iliq, esda qoladigan kompozitsiya.
- [Spark](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/spark/) — /design/spark/; Space Grotesk va DM Sans. Laym, moviy va pushti aksentlar; katta tipografika va jonli rang tanlash.
- [Open City](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/city/) — /design/city/; JetBrains Mono va DM Sans. Tiniq to‘q sariq, oq va yashil; texnik xarakterli, tartibli xizmat katalogi.
- [Bloom · Material Design 3](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/bloom/) — /design/bloom/; Rasmiy Material Web komponentlari, Material Symbols va dinamik rang tizimi. Ishlaydigan loyiha maydoni.
- [Yuksal · SI](https://azizbeksafarov.github.io/design/si/) — /design/si/; Oq asosiy fon, O‘zbekistonning moviy va yashil aksentlari; yarim oy va 12 yulduzli zarrachali bayroq. SI.gov harakat mexanikasi, serif va jonli shakl almashishi.
